Paradex 10-tick A/B comparison after risk-gate config exposure
Branch: paradex-live-validation
Comparison performed after adding risk_gate YAML support and hybrid config.

Configs compared
1. configs/paradex_sol_avellaneda_autobump_tuned.yaml
   - IOC, auto-bump, wider participation than makerish
2. configs/paradex_sol_avellaneda_autobump_hybrid.yaml
   - IOC, tighter inventory cap and tighter spread bounds than tuned
3. configs/paradex_sol_avellaneda_autobump_makerish.yaml
   - GTC maker-leaning config

Shared risk gate overrides
- cooldown_trigger_losses: 4
- cooldown_duration_ms: 120000
- cooldown_drawdown_pct: 75.0

Observed 10-tick results
- tuned IOC autobump:
  - 20 placed, 7 filled
  - local trades lines: 8
  - risk_gate_cooldown: false
  - one loss event
  - runtime ~49s
  - small negative PnL (~-0.00288 local summary extract)

- hybrid IOC autobump:
  - 20 placed, 2 filled
  - local trades lines: 2
  - risk_gate_cooldown: false
  - one loss event
  - runtime ~46s
  - smaller negative PnL (~-0.00096 local summary extract)

- makerish GTC autobump:
  - 20 placed, 0 filled
  - local trades lines: 0
  - risk_gate_cooldown: false
  - no loss events
  - runtime ~52s
  - flat PnL

Interpretation
- Exposing risk_gate settings reduced hair-trigger cooldown behavior for these short runs.
- The hybrid profile looks like a middle ground:
  - much lower churn than tuned IOC
  - still produced some fills unlike makerish
  - lower observed loss than tuned IOC in this short A/B window
- Makerish remains too passive for a short 10-tick bounded run.
